<p class="rtejustify"><a href="https://iohk.io/about/">IOHK</a>&nbsp;is a technology company commited to using peer-to-peer innovations to deliver decentralized financial services. The company is leading the&nbsp;<a href="https://iohk.io/projects/cardano/">Cardano project,</a>&nbsp;an ambitious effort to develop a 3rd generation cryptocurrency that can solve the scalability and interoperability needs of current cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin or Ethereum. Cardano is a decentralized application that runs over the Internet and other private IP networks, forming a secure overlay over many IP layers. As such, part of the tasks of the Cardano software is to manage the&nbsp;<a href="https://cardanodocs.com/technical/protocols/network-transport/">creation and efficient&nbsp;oepration of this overlay</a>.&nbsp;<a href="https://iohk.io/team/charles-hoskinson/">Charles Hoskinson</a>, CEO and one of the co-foudners of IOHK, announced plans to adopt RINA as a future-proof and scalable foundation to support the networking needs of Cardano.</p>
