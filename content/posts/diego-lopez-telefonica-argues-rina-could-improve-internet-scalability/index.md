<p class="rtejustify">Diego Lopez, in this&nbsp;<a href="http://blogthinkbig.com/rina/">post at the Telefonica “Think Big” blog</a>&nbsp;(in spanish), explains that the current Internet has reached a size that has exceeded the expectations of its original designers by orders of magnitued. In order to reach this size&nbsp;many patches have been “organically” incorporated into the architecture, making it more complex, hard to manage and very prone to failures. RINA could be the solution to provide scalability to the next generation Internet.</p>
