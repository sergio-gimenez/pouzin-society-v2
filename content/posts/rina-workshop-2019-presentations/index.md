<p class="rtejustify">The presentations of the papers at the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.icin-conference.org/RINA.php#program">RINA Workshop 2019</a>&nbsp;are now online!&nbsp;The event, which was co-located with the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.icin-conference.org/">ICIN 2019 conference</a>, was well attended by approximately 50 people that contributed to a lively discussion. The workshop featured a keynote on the RINArmenia initiative, an industry panel discussing challenges and opportunities for RINA adoption and a technical session with 8 papers.</p>
