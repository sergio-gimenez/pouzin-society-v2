<p class="rtejustify"><strong>OCARINA</strong>&nbsp;(Optimizations to Comple Adoption of RINA), is the name of a new research project from the University of Oslo (UiO) that has just been funded by the Norwegian government, under its&nbsp;<a href="http://www.forskningsradet.no/no/Nyheter/En_milliard_til_sterke_forskningsmiljoer/1254015683057?WT.mc_id=nyhetsbrev-ForskningsradetNorsk">Toppforsk program</a>. The project, led by Michael Welzl, has been awarded&nbsp;NOK 16 539 000 (approximately 1.75 M€) to investigate congestion control, routing and forwarding strategies for the Recursive InterNetwork Architecture (RINA), as well as deployment and adoption issues. OCARINA will have a duration of 5 years, starting October 2016. Congratulations to the UiO’s team!!!</p>
